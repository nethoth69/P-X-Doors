How it works

  1. 01

    Browse & configure

    Pick a door, then set its size and finish.

  2. 02

    Choose your service

    Self-fit, standard installation, or full installation with old-door removal — plus an after-service plan if you want one.

  3. 03

    We confirm

    Our team reviews your order and calls or WhatsApps you to confirm the details and a date.

  4. 04

    Installed to spec

    Delivered, or fitted by our team if you chose installation. Payment is arranged on the day.

  5. 05

    After-care

    If you added a care plan, we follow up to check hinges, locks and finish.
